## 1. Introduction & Background

### 1.1 Project Overview

Siyathemba Local Municipality, through its LED Unit, requires the design, development, implementation, hosting, maintenance, and digital marketing of the Prieska Digital Tourism Platform and Destination Marketing Ecosystem.

The platform will serve as the official digital home for tourism in the Siyathemba Municipal area — covering **Prieska**, **Marydale**, and **Niekerkshoop**. It must function as a compelling, modern, and fully integrated destination marketing portal connecting visitors with local experiences, accommodations, businesses, and events.

### 1.2 Background & Context

Prieska and the broader Siyathemba area possess significant, largely undiscovered tourism potential: natural landscapes, the Orange River, dark-sky stargazing, cultural heritage, hunting, agricultural tourism, and authentic Northern Cape experiences.

Despite this, the area currently has **near-zero digital visibility**. There is no centralised tourism platform for the region, local businesses lack digital marketing channels, visitor information is fragmented, and the region does not appear meaningfully in Northern Cape tourism search results.

### 1.3 Project Objectives

| Objective | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Tourism Promotion** | Promote Prieska, Marydale, and Niekerkshoop as distinctive Northern Cape destinations. Showcase attractions, outdoor adventures, heritage, cultural assets, and community experiences. Increase domestic and international visitor arrivals. |
| **Economic Development** | Provide local businesses with high-quality, affordable digital marketing channels. Support SMME development through premium listing tiers. Create sustainable tourism revenue for local entrepreneurs. |
| **Digital Transformation** | Establish a modern, scalable destination management platform. Improve accessibility and quality of tourism information. Enable seamless digital engagement between visitors, businesses, and the municipality. |
| **Destination Marketing** | Build a recognisable tourism brand identity. Achieve strong organic search visibility for Prieska tourism. Support integrated campaigns across search, social, and email. |

### 1.4 Platform Benchmark

Primary quality benchmark: **capetown.travel** — the official Cape Town Tourism destination marketing portal. The Prieska platform must meet or exceed this standard in user experience, visual design, content depth, business directory functionality, interactive mapping, AI assistance, and digital marketing integration.

Secondary reference: **experiencenortherncape.com** — the Experience Northern Cape provincial tourism portal, relevant for regional content and route structures.

## 4. Technical Requirements

### 4.1 Platform Architecture

No specific stack is prescribed, but these architectural standards must be met:

- Clear separation between frontend presentation layer and backend services
- RESTful or GraphQL API architecture for data access and third-party integration
- Database design supporting multi-tenancy for business profiles and content
- Scalable architecture capable of handling traffic growth without fundamental redesign
- API-first design to support future mobile application development
- Version-controlled codebase with documented branching and deployment strategy
- Staging and production environment separation
- Third-party integration capability — mapping APIs, AI services, email, payment gateways
- CMS integration (headless or traditional) for content management
- Modular architecture enabling independent feature updates and future expansion

### 4.2 Hosting & Infrastructure

| Requirement | Specification |
|---|---|
| Hosting | Cloud-based. South African or African data centre preferred for POPIA compliance and latency. Global CDN required. |
| Uptime SLA | Minimum 99.5% monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance |
| Maintenance Windows | Outside peak tourism traffic hours. Minimum 48-hour advance notice. |
| Domain Management | Full registration and DNS management, registered in the municipality's name |
| SSL / TLS | Valid certificates across all domains and subdomains, auto-renewal |
| CDN | Required for all static assets, images, and media |
| Backup | Daily automated backups. Minimum 30-day retention with off-site storage. |
| Disaster Recovery | Documented DR plan. RTO max 4 hours, RPO max 24 hours. |
| Monitoring | 24/7 automated uptime and performance monitoring with alerting. Monthly reports. |
| Scalability | Auto-scaling for traffic spikes during campaigns or viral content events |

### 4.3 Cybersecurity & Data Protection

**Authentication & Access Control**
- Secure user authentication with MFA for administrative accounts
- Role-based access control (RBAC) with principle of least privilege
- Secure password policies — minimum complexity, breach detection, reset workflows
- Session management with automatic timeout for inactive sessions
- OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect support for social login options
- Failed login attempt monitoring and account lockout policies

**Platform Security**
- TLS 1.2 minimum (TLS 1.3 preferred) for all data in transit
- Encryption at rest for all sensitive personal and financial data
- Protection against OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities (SQL injection, XSS, CSRF, etc.)
- Regular automated vulnerability scanning (minimum monthly)
- Annual third-party penetration testing — report shared with municipality
- Web Application Firewall (WAF) and DDoS protection
- Dependency vulnerability monitoring and timely patching

### 4.4 Analytics & Reporting

**Business Owner Analytics Dashboard**
- Profile views and listing impressions
- Click-through rates (website link, phone, directions, enquiry form)
- Enquiry volumes and source tracking
- Review scores and visitor feedback summary
- Promotional campaign performance metrics
- Exportable performance reports

**Platform-Wide Analytics**
- Integration with Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Google Search Console
- Visitor traffic, session data, and geographic origin tracking
- Top-performing attraction and destination pages
- Search trend monitoring — what visitors are looking for
- Booking and enquiry volume trends
- Campaign performance tracking
- All analytics data remains the property of Siyathemba Local Municipality

### 4.5 SEO & Digital Marketing

SEO and digital marketing are critical components, not optional extras.

**Technical SEO**
- Clean URL structures and canonical tag implementation
- XML sitemap generation and submission to Google Search Console
- Schema.org structured data markup (LocalBusiness, TouristAttraction, Event, etc.)
- Page speed optimisation meeting Core Web Vitals thresholds
- Mobile SEO compliance across all pages
- 404 error monitoring and redirect management

**Local SEO**
- Google Business Profile setup and optimisation for key attractions and the municipality
- Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data across all directories
- Local citation building across relevant South African tourism directories
- Review management strategy for Google and TripAdvisor
- Prieska-specific keyword research and content strategy

**Content Marketing**
- Destination storytelling content plan — minimum 2 blog articles per month for 12 months
- Visitor experience guides and curated itinerary content
- Seasonal campaign content (summer, school holidays, public holidays)

**Social Media Marketing**
- Social media channel strategy with platform rationale
- Content calendar framework for the municipality's social media team
- Paid social advertising strategy for visitor acquisition

**Paid Advertising**
- Google Ads campaign strategy and structure (Search and Display)
- Remarketing campaign strategy for return visitor acquisition
- Campaign budget recommendations and expected performance benchmarks

**Email Marketing**
- Newsletter system setup and branded template design
- Visitor nurturing email sequence
- Business directory member newsletter

### 4.6 Performance Standards

| Metric | Standard |
|---|---|
| Page Load Time — Mobile (4G) | Under 3 seconds |
| Page Load Time — Desktop | Under 2 seconds |
| Google PageSpeed Score — Mobile | Minimum 80 / 100 |
| Google PageSpeed Score — Desktop | Minimum 90 / 100 |
| Core Web Vitals — LCP | Under 2.5 seconds |
| Core Web Vitals — INP | Under 200ms |
| Core Web Vitals — CLS | Under 0.1 |
| Platform Uptime | 99.5% monthly minimum |
| Concurrent User Capacity | Minimum 500 concurrent users without degradation |
| Search Response Time | Under 1 second for directory and content searches |
| AI Assistant Response Time | Under 5 seconds for standard queries |

**Mobile-First Requirement:** The majority of South African internet users access the web via mobile. The platform must be designed mobile-first and perform optimally on low-bandwidth and 3G connections — critical for the Northern Cape rural context.

- Mobile-first responsive design across smartphones, tablets, and desktop
- Touch-optimised navigation, buttons, and interactive elements
- Compressed image delivery with lazy loading
- Minified CSS and JavaScript for fast mobile load times
- Progressive Web App (PWA) capability enabling offline content browsing (DESIRABLE)

## 6. Compliance & Ownership

### 6.1 POPIA Compliance

Full compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), Act 4 of 2013. A formal data processing agreement governs collection, processing, storage, and deletion of all personal information.

- Privacy Policy and Terms of Use compliant with POPIA
- Explicit, informed consent mechanisms for all personal data collection
- Data subject rights management — access, correction, and deletion requests
- Data retention policy documentation and technical enforcement
- Third-party data processor agreements for all external service integrations
- Data breach detection, notification within 72 hours, and documented response procedures
- The service provider acts as an operator and processes data only on municipal instruction
- Upon contract termination, all personal data is returned or securely destroyed as directed

### 6.2 B-BBEE & Procurement

Conducted in accordance with the PPPFA, the MFMA, and Siyathemba Local Municipality's Supply Chain Management Policy.

- Valid B-BBEE status level verification certificate from an accredited SANAS agency, or a sworn affidavit for EMEs and QSEs
- Certificate must be valid at submission and for the contract duration
- Joint ventures must submit consolidated B-BBEE certificates
- Sub-contracting arrangements must comply with PPPFA sub-contracting regulations
- Preference given to Siyathemba-area and Northern Cape-based enterprises, all else equal
- Service providers encouraged to demonstrate plans for local employment, skills development, and enterprise development within the Prieska area

### 6.3 Intellectual Property & Ownership

All intellectual property, platform assets, and data generated under this contract vest in Siyathemba Local Municipality.

- All custom-developed code, design assets, content, and digital deliverables become municipal IP upon final payment
- The municipality receives full source code, database schemas, design files, and deployment documentation at project completion and upon contract termination
- Third-party licensed components must be disclosed and documented; licences must permit commercial municipal use
- No proprietary lock-in preventing migration to another provider
- All domain names registered in the municipality's name and transferred without cost at contract termination
- The municipality retains unrestricted rights to all platform data — visitor data, analytics, and business directory data
- The service provider may not use platform content, branding, or data for marketing purposes without written municipal approval
